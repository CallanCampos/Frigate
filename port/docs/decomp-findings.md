the user decides whether a decomp session fixes a finding, as ordinary decomp work. The "equivalent" notes of

| `lib/nitro/include/nitro/snd.h:370` (`SNDDriverInfo`), read by `lib/nitro/src/snd/snd_work.c` (`SND_ReadTrackInfo`) | The guessed layout of the driver's state is wrong. The ARM7's `SND_ReadDriverInfo` (in `SND_CommandProc`, `0x03803a10`) copies its whole work, `SNDi_Work` at `0x03809fbc`, 0x1180 bytes: the 16 channels at 0 (0x54 bytes each), the 16 players at 0x540 (0x24), the 32 tracks at 0x780 (0x40), the 8 alarms at 0xF80 (0x40). After it come the 16 channel control words (`SND_GetChannelControl`) at 0x1180, the work's ARM7 address at 0x11C0 and the locked channels (`SND_GetLockedChannel`) at 0x11C4, in 0x11E0 bytes. The decomp has the players, the tracks' start and size, the address and the total right, but declares 20 tracks and puts the channels after them at 0xC80, where tracks 20 to 31 and the alarms are. The DS code is unaffected: `SND_ReadTrackInfo` indexes `track[]` with the driver's track number, 0 to 31, past the declared 20 at the right offsets, and reaches channels only through their ARM7 links, which point into `unk_0000` | `port/hal/snd7/snd7.c` (`snd7_read_driver_info`) fills the state by the decomp's names: the tracks the players use go into the 20 slots in player order with `trackIndex` renumbered (any past 20 read as none), the channels into `channel[]` with links as ARM7 addresses of that array, and the locked channels into `unk_11c4` | 2026-10-17, sound driver integration |
