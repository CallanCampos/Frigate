# The actors' event types, which the zone events give each NPC (ZoneNPC.evType, swan's EvType) and which
# field_actor_evtype.c's tables (swan's ACTOR_EVENTTYPE_FUNCS and the two after it) run by: whether the actor is a
# Trainer that looks for the player (EvType_GetSightType) and how it turns or hurries as it moves. The ones that
# nothing in the code tells apart are numbered. TRAINER: sees the way it faces. ALL_DIRS: sees every way. LOOK_AROUND:
# after it comes back to its first square a number of times (its second parameter), looks to either side.
# SPIN_COUNTERCLOCKWISE and SPIN_CLOCKWISE: then turns a full circle in place, up, left, down and right or up, right,
# down and left. WALK_SPIN_*: turns that way a quarter at each step it walks instead of facing its way. TURN_ON_B_PRESS:
# turns only when the player presses B beside it. MOVE_ON_B_PRESS: walks only when the player presses B beside it,
# for its second parameter's frames. DASH: walks, and LOOK_AROUND_DASH and SPIN_*_DASH turn, faster while the player
# holds B beside it. TURN_BACK_ON_SIGHT: turns back along its loop when it sees the player
